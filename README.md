## 🎥 What Is DynamicCrop?

DynamicCrop is a **dynamic video cropper for Android** designed for people who want more control over how their videos are framed.

A regular video cropper usually removes unwanted areas from a video using a fixed frame. DynamicCrop lets you adjust the crop area at different moments, allowing you to reframe footage as a subject moves or the desired composition changes.

With zoom, pan, rotation, and keyframe-based editing, you can turn existing footage into a dynamically cropped video without recording it again.

Whether you want to follow a moving person, zoom in on an object, or create a custom video composition, DynamicCrop gives you control over the visible area of your footage.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 Dynamic Video Cropping

Change the visible area of a video over time instead of applying only one fixed crop.

- Adjust the crop area at different points.
- Reframe footage as the subject moves.
- Create custom cropping movements.
- Export a video with your adjustments applied.

### 🔍 Video Zoom and Pan

Zoom into your footage and move the crop area to focus on the content that matters.

- Zoom in on people, objects, or other details.
- Pan across the video to change the framing.
- Reposition the crop area throughout the video.
- Create custom zooming and reframing effects.

### 🎞️ Manual Keyframe Editing

Use manual keyframes to control the crop area at specific points in the video.

Set different crop positions throughout the timeline to create a sequence of framing adjustments. This provides precise control over how the cropped video changes from one moment to another.

### 🤖 Automatic Keyframe Generation

Generate keyframes automatically to help track a selected subject throughout a video, where supported.

This feature can help reduce the amount of manual adjustment needed when reframing footage with a moving subject.

### 🔄 Video Rotation

Adjust video orientation as part of the editing and cropping process.

### 👁️ Preview Your Changes

Review your crop adjustments while editing and refine the framing before exporting the final video.

### 📤 Export Cropped Videos

Export your edited video with the applied cropping and framing adjustments.

## 🛠️ How to Crop and Reframe a Video

1. **Import a video** from your Android device.
2. **Choose your crop area** to select the part of the video you want to keep.
3. **Zoom and pan** to position the crop around your subject.
4. **Add keyframes** to change the crop area at different moments.
5. **Generate automatic keyframes**, where supported, to help track a selected subject.
6. **Preview your edits** and adjust the framing as needed.
7. **Export your video** with the new crop applied.
