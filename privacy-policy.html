DynamicCrop Privacy Policy

```

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Introduction

DynamicCrop is a video editing application that allows users to dynamically crop, zoom, pan, and adjust the framing of videos.

Video Access

DynamicCrop requires access to videos selected by the user in order to edit and process them.

User videos are processed locally on the device. DynamicCrop does not upload user videos to any external server.

Data Collection

DynamicCrop does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Permissions

Photo and video permissions are used only to allow users to select videos for editing and to save exported videos to the device.

Third-Party Services

DynamicCrop does not share user data or user videos with third parties.

Data Security

Since video processing is performed locally on the user's device, user videos are not transmitted to our servers.

Contact
```
